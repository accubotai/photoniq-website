1. The Problem: "Watts & Wafers"

The AI "supercycle" has hit a physical wall. Traditional electronic AI chips (GPUs) are bottlenecked by thermal limits, latency, and unsustainable power consumption.

Hyperscale data centers, LLM operators, and autonomous vehicle manufacturers are constrained by energy availability, not compute logic.

Incremental electronic node shrinking cannot bypass the fundamental physics limits of electron-based compute and copper interconnects.

2. The Solution: Computing with Light

Using passive, nanophotonic metasurfaces, light passes through our chips and performs complex neural-network inference instantaneously via interference and diffraction.

⚡

Zero-Energy Inference

Operations occur passively at the speed of light, entirely bypassing thermal bottlenecks and slashing data center power footprints.

📏

Subwavelength Density

Unprecedented compute density without bulky active phase-shifters, allowing for massive parallel processing in a microscopic footprint.

👁️

Zero-Latency Vision

Enables instantaneous machine vision for autonomous systems, processing visual data at the speed of light.

3. Why Now?
