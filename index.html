Multiplexed Optical Neural Networks

Based on our pending flagship patent (US20260073679A1), our chips utilize multiple wavelengths and polarizations of light simultaneously. A single microscopic PhotonIQ metasurface runs multiple distinct AI classification tasks in parallel.

Generative AI at the Physical Layer

Acting as physical decoders in Variational Autoencoders, our metasurfaces take random optical noise and physically bend it to generate structured output images, offering hardware-level generative AI without electronic lag.

🛡️

Unhackable Optical Encryption

Our metasurfaces are designed using machine-learning-optimized nanostructures, offering unprecedented physical-layer optical encryption. Data encoded in light is physically impossible to decode without the exact proprietary metasurface.
